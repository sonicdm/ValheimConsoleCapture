# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Mirror F5 console lines to `BepInEx/ValheimConsole.log`
- Rolling buffer with `copyconsole`, `dumpconsole`, and `clearconsolebuffer`
- `dumpcommand` runs one command or a quoted chain and writes only that output
- `consolecapture on/off`, optional rich-text stripping, and autocomplete capture
- Client-only; nothing is sent to other players
